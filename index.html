    Ingredients:

    • 1/2 cup soy sauce
    • 1/2 cup vinegar
    • 6 garlic cloves (crushed)
    • 3 bay leaves
    • 1 tsp peppercorns
    • 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
    Procedure:
    1. Marinate meat in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
